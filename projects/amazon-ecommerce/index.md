Star-schema analysis of Amazon BlissBaby sales (12 months): KPI tracking for Total Sales, Net Profit, ACOS, and TACOS at SKU and monthly levels.

## Highlights

- Power BI dashboard for sales, ad spend, and margin by AMZ SKU
- SQL views for cleaning, then DAX measures for ACOS / TACOS / net margin
- Peak TACOS and returns concentrated in Q4 (Oct–Nov)
